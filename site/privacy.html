Privacy

Your files stay on your device. PUB File Opener reads .pub files with code that runs in your browser; the file is never uploaded to us or anyone else.

We use cookieless analytics that count page visits. They don’t identify you or track you across sites.

If you buy a paid pass in the future, the payment is handled by our payment provider, which receives what it needs to process the order. We never see your card details.
